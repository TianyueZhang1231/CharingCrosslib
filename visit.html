Times may change on public holidays — please check with the library before travelling.

04無障礙

Access

We have partnered with WelcoMe so that you can share your access requirements with us before you arrive — discreetly, on your own terms, and without having to explain yourself at the door.

The redesign treats access as part of the atmosphere rather than an addition to it: wider aisles, seating at shorter intervals, and signage that can be read by touch as well as by eye.

01

Book your visit with WelcoMe

Tell the library what you need before you travel, and a member of staff will be ready.

Level access from Charing Cross Road, with a lift to every floor of the library.

05材料

What the room
is made of

The institutional library is usually built from stone and steel. This one is built from things that absorb sound and hold warmth: wood-fibre acoustic panel, woven textile, recycled paper and lime-washed plaster.

Every material was chosen to be repaired rather than replaced, and to age in a way that reads as use rather than damage.
